## What it is
Converting a Ford Transit: layout, furniture, electrical, and everything in between. TBD: model year and config (roof height, wheelbase), and what's already done.

## Stream angle
The most visual project on the channel. CAD on screen, then the real thing. Claude-maxxing angle: Claude does the electrical load and solar sizing math, cut lists, bill of materials and costing, and the build order. "-onomics": track the actual spend against a shop quote for the same build.

Stream modes:
- **Shop Floor (CAD):** design a cabinet, bed platform, or panel in Fusion live
- **Planning:** power budget, wiring diagram, BOM spreadsheet with Claude
- **Build day (IRL):** camera on the van. Needs a mobile or second-camera setup, so it's a later phase

## On-stream safety
- Don't show the plate, VIN, or your home or parking location on camera.
